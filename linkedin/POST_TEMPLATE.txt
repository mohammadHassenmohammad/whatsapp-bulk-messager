Why pay $50–$300/month just to send WhatsApp messages to your own customers? 🤷‍♂️

Most commercial platforms (WATI, Intercom, Twilio):
❌ Charge heavy monthly subscription fees
❌ Charge per-message surcharges ($0.03 - $0.08 per text)
❌ Force you to upload sensitive customer phone numbers to third-party cloud servers
❌ Rely on fragile AI wrappers that hallucinate

I wanted something completely private, reliable, and 100% free.

So I built WhatsApp Campaign Manager & Local CRM 🚀

Here is what makes it different:
🔒 100% Local-First: All customer data, chat history, and campaign queues live strictly on your machine in SQLite (127.0.0.1). Zero cloud data leakage.
⚡ Dual-Mode Outreach:
   1️⃣ Automated Baileys Gateway: Multi-device QR pairing with humanized jitter delays (8–15s) for anti-ban protection.
   2️⃣ Click-to-Chat Mode: 100% risk-free official WhatsApp Web redirection (wa.me).
💬 Real-Time 2-Way CRM Inbox: Inbound replies sync automatically with lead stages (Lead ➡️ Qualified ➡️ Proposal ➡️ Customer).
🛡️ Compliance & Opt-Out Engine: Automated detection of "STOP" keywords that auto-suppresses contacts to protect your phone number.
⚙️ Zero AI Bloat: Pure deterministic rule-based logic for predictable business communication.

🛠️ The Tech Stack:
• Backend: Python, FastAPI, SQLite (ACID with FK enforcement)
• Frontend: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Recharts
• Gateway: Node.js, @whiskeysockets/baileys (Multi-device protocol)
• Tests: 34 automated unit/integration tests (pytest)

It’s completely open-source under the MIT License!
